- We welcome motivated postdoctoral researchers, visiting students, and research interns to join or visit our group. If interested, please get in touch via email.

- Please note: I review all inquiries but **may not be able to respond individually**. I will contact you if there is a potential fit with our group, thank you for your understanding!

- (**Externally Funded Postdoctoral Positions**) If you are interested in conducting postdoctoral research in my group with external funding, here are some potential funding opportunities:
  - [Marie-Curie Postdoctoral Fellowships](https://ec.europa.eu/research/mariecurieactions/)
  - [FWF ESPRIT Career Advancement for Postdocs](https://www.fwf.ac.at/en/research-funding/fwf-programmes/esprit-programme)
